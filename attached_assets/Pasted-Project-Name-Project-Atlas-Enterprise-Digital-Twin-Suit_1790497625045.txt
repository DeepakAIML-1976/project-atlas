What makes this different
Most current AI assistants focus on isolated tasks:
- Meeting transcription
- Document search
- Chatbots
- Email drafting
- Workflow automation
Your concept unifies these capabilities into a persistent digital workforce. Every agent shares the same enterprise memory and collaborates through a common orchestration layer. Instead of dozens of disconnected copilots, the organization gains a coordinated network of AI employees that understand context, preserve institutional knowledge, and work together under governance.
My recommendation
Based on everything we've developed together—from your engineering AI systems, RAG architecture, schedule risk analysis, technical bid evaluation, document intelligence, lessons learned platform, and interest in autonomous AI employees—I would position this as a global enterprise software platform, not an industry-specific application.
The oil & gas domain should be your first vertical, where your deep expertise provides a competitive advantage. However, the underlying platform should be architected as an industry-agnostic Enterprise Digital Twin Suite from day one. Industry knowledge should be delivered through modular domain packs, allowing expansion into construction, manufacturing, aerospace, defence, pharmaceuticals, and beyond without redesigning the core platform.
I believe this has the potential to become an enterprise platform comparable in scope to modern ERP or collaboration systems, with Microsoft Teams serving as one of several user interfaces rather than the product itself.
The next logical step is to produce a complete production-grade blueprint, including a Product Requirements Document (PRD), Software Requirements Specification (SRS), system architecture, multi-agent architecture, knowledge graph schema, data model, API specifications, security architecture, UX designs, deployment architecture, implementation roadmap, and commercialization strategy. That blueprint would serve as the foundation for designing and building the full Enterprise Digital Twin Suite.
